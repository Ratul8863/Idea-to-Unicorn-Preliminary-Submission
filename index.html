ⓘ YouTube Video Guidance — “Vibe to Production in 180 Seconds”

Idea to Unicorn Pitch Standard. The preliminary submission is your first proof of execution — not an idea pitch. Show that your team can move from vibe (concept) to production thinking (working AI system) in a clear, structured 3-minute (180-second) video.

Your goal: Show that your idea is real, your AI approach is valid, and your team can build it.

🎬 180-Second Breakdown

TimeSegmentWhat You Must ShowKey MessageEvaluators Look For
0:00–0:30Problem (The Vibe)Define problem, target users, urgency (Bangladesh + global)“This problem matters”Clarity, relevance
0:30–1:00SolutionExplain your AI-driven solution and differentiation“This is how we solve it”Simplicity, uniqueness
1:00–2:00Demo / Concept FlowEarly prototype, mock flow, or system walkthrough“This is how it works”Feasibility, logic
2:00–2:30AI ApproachModels, RAG, data, personalization, tools“This is real AI thinking”Depth, structure
2:30–3:00Impact & Next StepValue, potential users, what comes next“We can build and scale this”Vision, potential

✅ Mandatory Requirements

  • 3-minute (180s) structured video pitch
  • Clear problem and user definition
  • AI-native approach (LLM / RAG / Graph / ML)
  • Basic system flow (input → AI → output)
  • Initial demo, prototype, or workflow (early-stage OK)
  • Consideration for Bangla / localization
  • Defined potential impact (KPIs or expected outcomes)

🏆 What Gets You Selected

Clarity: simple, easy-to-understand
AI Thinking: clear reasoning behind AI usage
Feasibility: realistically buildable
Structure: logical flow of explanation
Energy: confidence and ownership

⚠ Common Mistakes to Avoid

Submitting only an idea with no system thinking
No clear AI explanation
Overcomplicating instead of focusing
No demo or workflow visualization
Ignoring real users or real problems

🎯 Final Mindset

This is not about being perfect. It is about being clear, real, and buildable. You are not being judged on completion — you are being judged on your ability to execute.

“Vibe to Production in 180 Seconds — Show us you can build.”
